Crab Cove privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Crab Cove is a game made by Goo Holdings Ltd ("we"). This policy explains what the game records, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Crab Cove app for Android, including test builds.

The short version

  • We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and the game cannot read them.
  • If you choose Help improve on the first screen, the game sends us anonymous gameplay records and crash reports. If you choose No thanks, it sends nothing. You can change this at any time in Settings.
  • We use one service provider, Sentry, to receive and store those records. We do not sell or share your data, and we use it for nothing else.
  • The game has no third-party advertising. The "adverts" in the game are made up by us. They are not served by an ad network and they do not track you.

What the game records

When you have chosen Help improve, the game sends:

  1. A random install id. The game makes up a random number the first time it starts. It is not linked to your Google account, phone number, advertising id or any hardware id. Uninstalling the game removes it.
  2. Gameplay events: for example, a level started, won or lost, a tower built, a wave cleared, an item bought with in-game coins or gems, or a pretend advert watched. Each record has the time, the level, simple counters (such as days since install and session number), and which version of game settings you have. We use these records to find levels that are too hard or too easy and parts of the game that confuse players.
  3. Device information: the phone model (for example "Pixel 8"), the Android version, the game version, and your country as set in the phone's language settings (for example "GB"). We use it to fix problems that only happen on some phones.
  4. Crash and error reports: what went wrong in the game's code, and the last few gameplay events before it happened.

The game also keeps the same gameplay records in a file on your phone, so it can send them later if you are offline. That file stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

We do not collect your IP address on purpose. Our provider receives it as part of any internet connection. We have set it not to store IP addresses, and it may use the connection only to estimate your country.

If you choose No thanks, nothing above is sent. The game still works fully.

Who processes the data

Records are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io). Sentry stores them for us under its Data Processing Agreement, as our processor; we are the controller. Sentry may store data in the European Union.

How long we keep it

Sentry keeps crash reports for up to 90 days and gameplay records for up to 30 days, then deletes them. Anything we export for analysis is kept only as totals that cannot be traced back to an install.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn it off: Settings > Help improve > Off. The game stops sending at once and clears any records still waiting on your phone.
  • Delete what was sent: email us at crabcove@lineman.io with roughly when you played and your phone model, and we will find and delete those records within 30 days.
  • If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to access, correct or object to the processing of data about you, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

Crab Cove is made for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Purchases

The test build has no real-money purchases. If we add them, Google Play will handle the payment, and we will update this policy first.

Changes

If we change what the game records, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version reaches players.
