Crab Cove support

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Crab Cove is a game made by Goo Holdings Ltd. If something is not working, or you have a question about the game, we are glad to help.

Contact us

Email crabcove@lineman.io. Tell us what happened, which level you were on, and what phone you use. We aim to reply within three working days.

Common questions

  • The game closed or froze. Close it fully and open it again. If it keeps happening, email us with the level number and your phone model.
  • My progress is missing. Progress is saved on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it. If progress vanished without either, email us.
  • How do I turn off gameplay reporting? In the game, open Settings, then Help improve, and choose Off. The game works the same either way. Our privacy policy explains what is recorded.
  • A level feels impossible. Every level is built to be won without paying. Try a different tower on the pad nearest the gate, upgrade the hut early, and use an Island Power when the wave bunches up. If a level still seems broken, tell us its number.
  • I want my data deleted. Email us and we will delete the records sent from your install. The privacy policy explains how.
